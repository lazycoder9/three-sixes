    Kitchen Table

    A game played at home with whatever was in the drawer. The table and the pieces are wood: blocks you press, letter tiles for the Room code, draughts pieces for people, bone dice. Anything you read or fill in is a page torn from a notebook and written by hand. A pencil, masking tape and a sticky note are the only props. Light is the table in daylight. Dark is the same table in the evening with one lamp on, and the paper stays paper.
