How a Round goes

  1. Roll in secretEveryone starts with one die under their own cup.
  2. Bid, or RaiseSay how many of one face are on the whole table. The next Player has to Raise it or Check it.

    Timur two fours Malika three sixes!

  3. CheckCups up. Whoever was wrong takes a Penalty die. Take your sixth and you're Knocked out.
