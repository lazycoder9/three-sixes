      A modern game box in daylight. Everything is built from the pip, a circle on a three-by-three grid. People are pips, stats are pips, and a Bid is written as a count and a die face. Each screen gets one flat colour field: cobalt for the game, mango for a Room, ink for records. Mango marks whose turn it is, coral is the Check.
