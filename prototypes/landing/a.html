36s is a dice-bluffing game for a Room of friends. Nobody sees your dice. Everybody hears your Bid.

  1. Roll in secretEveryone starts with one die under their own cup.
  2. Bid, or RaiseSay how many of one face are on the whole table. The next Player Raises it or Checks it.
  3. CheckCups up. Whoever was wrong takes a Penalty die. Take your sixth and you're Knocked out.
