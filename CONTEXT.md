# Three Sixes

An online, real-time dice-bluffing game (a Liar's Dice variant) for playing with teammates and friends in private rooms.

## Language

**Three Sixes**:
The game and the product, named after the Bid "three sixes". Written out in words wherever the name is text. The logo writes it as a Bid: 3 × ⚅, a three, a times sign and a die showing six.
_Avoid_: 36s, 3×6, 666, Liar's Dice, Believe or Not

**Bid**:
A claim about all dice on the table, such as "three sixes": at least that many dice show that face. Ones are ordinary faces, never wild.
_Avoid_: Claim, call

**Raise**:
Making a Bid higher than the current one: a higher count of any face, or the same count of a higher face. Ones are the lowest face.
_Avoid_: Believe, outbid

**Check**:
Calling the current Bid to test it, which reveals every Player's dice and ends the Round.
_Avoid_: Challenge, doubt, liar, dudo

**Bluff caught**:
A Check that exposes a false Bid. It counts for the bidder as a Bluff caught and for the Checker as a Check won.
_Avoid_: Busted, called out

**Checker**:
The Player who made the Check. Only the Player on turn may Check.

**Player on turn**:
The one Player who may act right now, by Raising or Checking.

**Round**:
One cycle of rolling, Bids and Raises, ending in a Check.

**Blind**:
A Player who has chosen not to see their own dice this Round and Bids without looking, under the same rules as everyone. The choice is a switch that holds from Round to Round until turned off, and a blind Player may peek, which shows their dice for the rest of the Round and tells everyone they peeked.
_Avoid_: Dark, hidden hand

**Game**:
A sequence of Rounds that ends when one Player is left who is not Knocked out.
_Avoid_: Match

**Penalty die**:
The extra die the loser of a Check takes: the Checker if the Bid stood, otherwise the bidder. A Check on three sixes (exactly 3 × ⚅) costs two Penalty dice. Every Player starts a Game with one die.

**Knocked out**:
The state of a Player who has reached six dice or more, at that moment, and plays no further in the Game.
_Avoid_: Eliminated, dead

**Placement**:
A Player's finishing position in a Game: the winner first, then the others in reverse order of being Knocked out. A Player who leaves a Game mid-way is Knocked out at that moment.
_Avoid_: Rank, score

## Rooms

**Room**:
A private space where friends gather to play a series of Games, entered with a Room code. It closes after 15 minutes with nobody connected.
_Avoid_: Lobby, table, session

**Room code**:
The four-letter code that identifies an open Room, such as KQXT. It can be reused once the Room closes.
_Avoid_: Room number, room ID

**Host**:
The one person in a Room who can start Games and remove people. At first this is whoever created the Room.
_Avoid_: Admin, owner

**Player**:
Someone dealt into the current Game. A Knocked-out Player keeps their Placement but watches as a Spectator.
_Avoid_: Participant

**Spectator**:
Someone in the Room who is not playing the current Game: a late arrival, a Knocked-out Player, or someone sitting it out. Sees only what is public, never hidden dice.
_Avoid_: Observer, watcher

**Away**:
The state of someone in the Room with no working connection right now: a locked phone, lost network or closed tab. Being Away is not leaving; only the Leave Room button counts as leaving.
_Avoid_: Offline, disconnected, AFK

**Reaction**:
A one-tap emoji or canned line from a fixed set that pops up next to a person's seat or name for a few seconds. It is never kept.
_Avoid_: Chat, message, emote

**Tally**:
The count of Games each person has won since the Room opened. It ends when the Room closes.
_Avoid_: Scoreboard, leaderboard

## People

**Guest**:
A person known only by this device, with no account. Guests can play everything but have no stats or history on show.
_Avoid_: Anonymous, visitor

**Account**:
A person signed in with Google. An Account keeps its nickname, seat, stats and Game history across devices, and takes over the Guest history of the device it signs in on.
_Avoid_: User, profile, member

**Nickname**:
The name a person goes by in a Room. It is unique within the Room: a repeated name automatically gets a number, such as "Dana 2", which its owner can edit.
_Avoid_: Username, handle, display name
